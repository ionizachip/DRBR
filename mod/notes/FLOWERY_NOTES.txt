KNIGHT HUB build 50 - FLOWERY: implementation notes
===================================================
These notes answer the Flowery rebuild spec (part 15): the asset manifest, the character-slot audit and the ability docs.
TESTING_REPORT.txt says what was actually tested and what was not.
Short version: everything here was checked by building the data.win and by running emulator tests (a GML interpreter that
runs the hub's own code). NOTHING was played in the real game. How he looks and sounds on a real screen is not checked.

1. CHARACTER SLOT
-----------------
Flowery is character 5, his own slot. He is not Noelle (4) and not a Noelle reskin; he and Noelle can be in one party.
His battle hero is obj_knight_herocc. Its parent is obj_heroparent, the game's own hero logic. All of his data lives in
one registry entry (cc50.gml: knight_cc_register). That covers his poses, frame counts, face, voice, HP colour, gear,
techniques and stat script. The registry takes ids 5 and up, so a future custom character is one more entry like his
(see flowery50.gml: knight_fl_register).

2. SLOT AUDIT: places where he used another character, what changed, and why
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 a. Battle hero. Before, obj_heroparent's Create gave every hero Kris's sprites, and SPELL / SPELLREADY were Ralsei's
    (spr_ralseib_*). That is why he "shifted into Ralsei" when casting. Now knight_cc_dress sets every pose (idle, walk,
    ready, attack, act, spellready, spell, item, defend, hurt, defeat, victory) and the frame counts. It runs again
    whenever a pose is not his.
    The SECOND place it came from (fixed in this update): scr_spellconsumeb copies remspellsprite back into spellsprite
    on every cast, and obj_heroparent's Create had filled remspellsprite with Ralsei's Chapter 1 spell pose
    (spr_ralseib_spell) before his poses were put on. knight_cc_dress now sets remspellsprite / remspellframes too.
    Checked: t_flowery50_cc compares his hero's sprites to spr_knfl_* for every knight variant.
 b. Build 50's first version used Noelle's slot (4) and her hero. Now he has his own id (5) and obj_knight_herocc.
    The Flowery-on-Noelle code paths were removed from rules / b38 / b39 / weird.
 c. Follower and leader in the cold place. The game's caterpillar built number 5 as Berdly, or as nobody. Now
    knight_cc_follower and knight_cc_cater_dress make him follow as himself, and the leader walks as him.
    Checked: t_online_flowery50.
 d. HP box. scr_charbox drew Noelle's head and name and the default colour. Now knight_cc_charbox draws his own head,
    name and golden colour (189950). It applies to every version of scr_charbox in the game.
 e. FIGHT bar. obj_attackpress used Noelle's bar colour and head. Now it uses his gold bar and his own head
    (spr_knfl_pressfront).
 f. FIGHT hit. obj_heroparent ran Noelle's / Kris's hit effect and sound. Now it uses his crescent hit (spr_knfl_hitfx)
    and swing sound.
 g. Damage-number colour. Before it was the "type = char - 1" of whoever had that number. Now it comes from
    knight_cc_dmtype.
 h. Text: face, typer and voice. Before, his lines had no face of his own, so the game fell back to Ralsei / Noelle
    style typers and snd_text. Now the battle's spell-text writer is made with his face (spr_face_flowery, fc 23), his
    expression and his voice typer (302: his Chapter 5 blips snd_flowery_voicenoise_1-3) from the start
    (knight_cc_battletext, hooked into obj_spellphase). Switching the face on mid-text (the first way this was done)
    put only the first line beside the face and wrapped the rest under it - that is the garbled box that was reported.
    A narration line ("Everyone's TP went up!") is its own page after his line is closed: no face, the battle's
    normal text voice (knight_cc_narrate).
 i. Techniques, gear and info. scr_spellinfo / scr_spell / scr_spelltext / scr_weaponinfo / scr_armorinfo now hand ids
    16-39 and gear ids 30-35 to the registry. Nothing of his is in another character's tables.
 j. Hub knight switches (Tag Team / Remix / online). His hero was put away as one of the knight's own objects
    (obj_knight_*). That is why he vanished during the Violet and Thorn fights and online. Fixed in knight_lu_owned.
 k. Online kit. kfo_kit_apply read a 5-entry table at index 5, which crashed the game. Fixed (the table is guarded).
 l. Pronouns: "him" for 5. knight_hero_obj(5) returns obj_knight_herocc.
Not changed: the game's own global arrays still have room for 20 characters, so nothing was resized there.

4. ABILITIES
------------
TP is out of 250. AT / MAG are his battle stats. Damage = ceil(AT*a + MAG*m - foe DF*3), times the knight's damage
reduction + 0.65 (the same as the game's spells), at least 1, and 0 if it misses. 1 frame = 1/30 s.
Network: online matches use the room's kit (no techniques, no items), so none of these are cast online. Online players
see his sprites as a SKIN only. Nothing is sent over the network for them.
Completion: every move is a timed sequence (knight_fl_kspell_tick). It removes all its markers, puts his hero back and
stops its sounds when it ends. If the fight ends mid-move, or a move runs past 600 frames, it is cleaned up at once.
The battle's spelldelay is always longer than the move.

   voice: give_to_you / leaf_it_to_me / try_my_flavor / thatsgreat, in turn). Animation: the pointing pose with a flash,

5. NOT DONE / LIMITS (honest)
-----------------------------
 - Omega shader: Chapter 5's shd_chromatic_reflection is not ported. A shader is compiled code inside data.win, and
   copying one between game versions can stop the game from starting, so it was left out. The rainbow tint is a
   stand-in.
 - Speech bubbles: his lines use the battle text box with his face, typer and voice, not Chapter 5's speech balloons.
 - Online: his techniques are not part of online matches (the kit has none). Not tested with several real players.
 - Nothing here was seen or heard in the running game.

6. WEIRD ROUTE / VIOLET KNIGHT WITH FLOWERY (this update)
--------------------------------------------------------
 - The party-order rules count him like the others. On top, he stays to the end, and the Knight takes the members
   below him (Kris too). At the bottom, he is taken first. Alone, he is the last member and parries the blade.
   Before this, he was invisible to these rules: with Flowery on top and Kris below, Kris was treated as "on top"
   and Kris did the parry.
 - The parry and the power-up flurry show his own frames on his own hero. Before, the game's act-sprite helper fell
   back to Kris.
 - The talk after a loss: he speaks in a speech balloon like Susie's, in his own text voice.
   Shock: "...Kris?" / "Hey.. get up.. That's not funny." Resolve: "...Hey. Nobody hurts my team on my watch."
   The others call out to him when he is taken: Susie "F-Flowery...?", Noelle "F-Flowery...?!".
 - The Violet Knight calls him "The Flower Man" in its lines ("... was completely obliterated", "... stands alone"),
   the same way it has The Cage / The Girl / The Prince / The Angel. Everywhere else he is Flowery.
 - His finisher when he is on top: LAST JARONA (technique 23). After the power-up scene his MAGIC is only it and
   PRISM RUSH. 100 TP (40%), always. Damage AT*18 + MAG*6 over three rushes (30% / 25% / 45%), with explosions at the foe.
   After the Knight's Roaring it ends the fight the same way the other finishers do (the Knight's death scene).

7. THE THREE NEW SPECS (Prism Rush visuals, Jarona, last stand / ending)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
7.1 How Chapter 5 draws Omega Flowery and the flowers (found in its code, obj_plat_finalflowery)
 - Omega Flowery: scr_draw_outline(2, hue) - his sprite as one solid colour, 2 px out in 4 directions, the hue cycling
   (hsvslider += 0.3 a frame, make_color_hsv((hsvslider / 14) * 255 mod 255, 255, 255)); then his white sprite at
   alpha 0.8; then the same sprite again ADDITIVE through the shader shd_chromatic_reflection (iAlpha 0.5).
 - shd_chromatic_reflection (its GLSL source, read from Chapter 5's file): per pixel, hue = 0.5 + frac((iTime.x +
   (fragX + fragY) * 0.01) * 0.5), saturation 1, brightness 3.7 x (angle term), times the sprite's alpha - a rainbow
   running diagonally across the SCREEN, shown only where the sprite is.
 - The flowers (draw_ghost): the OVERLAY sprite (spr_omegaf_<name>_o - the solid shape behind; this is the outline
   region the sprite sheet's notes describe) in the flower's colour darkened 20%; the MAIN sprite (spr_omegaf_<name>:
   white with grey detail tones) in #EEEEEE mixed half with the colour; then the same additive shader pass.
   Colours: Green 7864183, Aqua 16777079, Yellow 7864319, Blue 16742263, Seth 16742399, Orange 7829503 (GameMaker
   BGR: #77FF77, #77FFFF, #FFFF77, #7777FF, #FF77FF, #FF7777).
 - Its explosions: spr_omegaflowery_explosion_a/b/c, white, turning a random flower hue after 2 or 4 frames; its
   "bwoosh" ripples (scr_make_ripple) in flower hues at radii 440 / 330 / 220 for the big hit.
7.2 What was built (obj_knight_omega, omega50.gml)
 - The same three layers, in the same order, with the same colours. The shader itself is NOT copied: Chapter 3's game
   file does not have it, and copying a compiled shader between game files can stop the game from starting. Its
   rainbow is made the same way without it: the sprite is drawn into a surface, the diagonal rainbow (the shader's own
   formula, 16 px bands) is multiplied in by the sprite's alpha, and the surface is added to the screen at 0.45.
   It keeps every pixel of the sprite's shape (no rectangle, nothing outside the sprite), and the grey detail tones
   stay visible because the base sprite is drawn separately. The angle term of the shader is taken as a constant.
 - Omega Flowery's frames: spr_knfl_omega (27 frames: pose, power-up, rush, clash, wind-up, punch, idle, hurt,
   deflect), lined up on his body like his normal poses.
 - A preview of the method, drawn outside the game with the same steps (see the delivery message), matches the
   formation of your first reference picture. It is NOT a screenshot of the game.
7.3 Prism Rush (the phases, frames at 30 fps): 1-49 he steps into the middle and kneels, powering up; the six Omega
   flowers glide in one by one to their places in the reference's formation (Aqua upper left, Blue top, Green upper
   right, Seth left, Yellow right, Orange below). 50-79 they hover (Chapter 5's own hover speeds), sparkles of their
   colours drifting into him. 80-99 they glow full; a ripple from each. 100-149 one by one they flash, rush into him
   shrinking, trailing their overlay in their colour, and are gone (a small ring and yo_hit2 as each arrives).
   150-169 he rises pointing up; six ripples ring out from him. 170-189 OMEGA FLOWERY ("Omega Flowery!").
   190-365 the attack: power-up with afterimages in the outline hue; three rushes - at each contact an explosion
   (white, then a hue), a ripple and a white orb at the foe, and the hit; the clash (Flowery alone - Kris no longer jumps in); the wind-up
   (three rings closing in); the punch - three explosions, the 220/330/440 ripples, an orb, a shake, the last hit;
   then his Omega form fades and he is himself again in his place. Damage and TP as before (100%, AT*12 + MAG*4.5;
   hits 15% x3, 20%, the rest). spelldelay 380 (190 when he is already Omega Flowery for good: no transformation,
   no change back).
7.4 My Jarona: three charges (25% / 30% / 45%). Each: wind-up with a white flash and "Jarona!", a 5-frame lunge in

   Jarona." spelldelay 125.
7.5 FIGHT: one Jarona. The game's own FIGHT (the attack bar, its damage, its one hit) is kept; what you see is his
   Jarona timed on it: wind-up with a white flash (0-5), the lunge (6-11) reaching the foe on the frame the game's
   hit lands (obj_heroparent deals it 11 frames after the swing starts), a blue flash and a shockwave there, knocked
   back (15-26).
   About "hit detection": Deltarune's battles have no collision between a hero and the enemy - every hit is dealt
   by the battle code at a set moment. So the moves are timed so that the visible contact and the damage happen on
   the same frame; there is no hitbox to tunnel through, and no hit can land twice.
7.6 His last stand (every knight's hub fight; not online): at the end of an enemy turn, when Flowery is up and

   Once per fight; it all goes when the fight ends. Speed +10% from the spec is not done (Deltarune battles have no
