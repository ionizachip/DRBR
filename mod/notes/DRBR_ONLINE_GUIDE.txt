KNIGHT FIGHTS ONLINE - build 50 guide
=====================================
A battle royale for 2 to 8 players inside the Knight Hub (DELTARUNE Chapter 3): every player dodges the SAME Knight, the
SAME attacks, at the SAME moment, in their own copy of the battle. The last SOUL standing wins. Up to 4 more room members
can WATCH a match (WATCH PLACES).
(KNIGHT page > ONLINE, or the sign in the cold place while you are in a room.)

How it works (the short version)
--------------------------------
- One shared match timeline: the host's game picks a seed; every game works out from it, by itself, which knight comes,
  which attack it uses, when it starts, and the randomness inside the attack. Nothing about the attacks has to travel
  over the network, so every screen shows the same knight and attack at the same time even with lag.
- Your screen is yours: your SOUL, your hits, your HP, your gauge and skills are decided in your game. Attacks that AIM at
  a SOUL aim at YOUR SOUL on your screen; everything that is not aimed lands in the same places for everyone. A screen
  shake never moves a bullet online (build 47).
- The host passes the players' states around (the other SOULs, HP, outs, colours) and only steps in when something does
  not add up (a game out of step is put back in step; impossible numbers are corrected); the host decides outs, places
  and the winner.
- The Cyan Knight (the joke fight) is never online. Offline it is exactly as it was. (Cyan as a SOUL COLOUR is fine.)

The room
--------
Left: the players with their skin, SOUL colour, name, status (HOST, READY, REMATCH, ALIVE, OUT #n, WATCHING) and ping;
more than 8 places (watchers) in shorter rows. Right: START (host) or READY / REMATCH (your vote after a match), SPECTATE
MATCH and JOIN MATCH while a match runs (greyed with the reason when you cannot), SKIN, SOUL (< > colour, Z the page),
KEYBINDS, MORE OPTIONS, SETTINGS, INVITE FRIENDS (Steam), COPY CODE, CONNECTION HELP, REMOVE A PLAYER (host), LEAVE ROOM.
C starts the match for the host from anywhere on the page. Under the rows: the preset, the knights, the mutators and what
the room is doing ("MATCH ON 1:20. 3 OF 5 STANDING, 1 WATCHING.", "REMATCH: 2 OF 3 VOTED").
THE COLD PLACE IS THE LOBBY: closing the menu keeps you in the room (only a PRACTICE room ends). In a room:
  - nobody's party follows them (Susie / Ralsei are put to sleep, not removed: they come back when you leave the room);
  - you look like your SKIN, and so does everyone else (walking, standing, the four ways); names over heads (ROOM NAMES);
  - the way east to the original Knight encounter is closed by a wall ("The way forward is blocked during online play.")
    and the encounter itself refuses to start while you are in a room - the online matches are the room's fights;
  - the box top left shows the room code, how many are ready, what to do; the sign opens the room page.
When the host leaves (or their game drops off) the next player takes the room over - in a match too (watchers keep
watching, and are told who hosts now).

The match
---------
Countdown, then FIGHT!: the shared timeline runs. The knight changes between attacks as the room's SWITCHING says; STAGE
RAMP moves the match through the stages (0 PURE, 1 GUESTS, 2 SHUFFLE, 3 KNIGHTMARE: the Twin Knight joins, 4 OVERDRIVE:
Hyper speed). The online knights: NORMAL, KAIZO, ELECKNIGHT, VIOLET, THORN and KNIGHTMARE.
SUDDEN DEATH, FINAL TWO, TIE RULE, TIME LIMIT: as in build 46 (the room's settings).
The other SOULs are drawn at 70% by default (V switches them off / on; F > SOUL OPACITY slides them). A banner tells
you about outs and the lead (OUT NOTICES), MATCH INFO shows the cycle, players, time, preset and sudden death, NETWORK
STATS the ping and lost pings; "NO NEWS FROM THE HOST" / "NEW HOST" warnings show when they apply.
Outs: your HP runs out -> OUT (#place); your SOUL cracks and shatters as in Chapter 3's GAME OVER, then the GAME OVER
shows over the battle (which goes on for the others) with your place, fades, and you watch a living player. Another
player's SOUL cracks and shatters where it was, in their colour - no GAME OVER for anyone else.
THE ROARING: the first attack of the final two (two SOULs left - with FINAL TWO off too, without its showdown) and the
first attack that starts once the match has run 3 minutes are the Roaring of whichever knight has that attack (Roaring
DELTA for Kaizo). It is long (about 27-40 seconds); HOLD BREATH is two speeds faster during it.

TARGETING (the room's SETTINGS > TARGETING; on by default)
---------------------------------------------------------
The command row under the box shows the seven modes; yours is yellow with the SOUL. It only decides where YOUR attack
skills (Sword, Flurry, the mid-pool ones, Knightmare) go - drawing and using skills is unchanged.
  RANDOM (build 50: CHAOS)      a random player still standing, a new one after every send (the default)
  LOW HP (PROCEED)       the player with the least HP
  CRYSTALS (BIG SHOT)    the player with the most shards
  REVENGE (DEFEND)       everyone targeting you, the send split between them (nobody on you: RANDOM)
  CHOOSE (CHECK)         the player you pick: 1-8 = the number in the player list, A = the next one (both switch to CHECK)
  TIRED (PACIFY)         the TIRED players (healed in the last 4 seconds, or an empty gauge), split between them (none: RANDOM)
  EVERYONE (ROARING)     every other player at once, at half strength each
A target that falls or leaves is replaced at once by the mode's rule (the host decides every target and every knockout).
A send starts around its target's SOUL (in every game: the target's own SOUL in their game, where the host last saw it
everywhere else), aimed swords go for that SOUL, and its bullets hurt ANYONE who touches them except the sender. They are
tinted in the sender's colour (the colour square of the player list).
SHARDS: a knockout gives the player whose skill landed the final hit 1 Shadow Crystal shard plus half the fallen player's
shards (rounded down); a Knight's hit is credited to the last player who hurt them. Shards make your sends stronger:
  0-1 shards x1.00   2-3 x1.15   4-7 x1.30   8-15 x1.50   16+ x1.75 (damage; SLOW / CONFUSE last longer, up to x1.5)
DEFEND / PACIFY on several players: each gets 1/n of the send (never under 40%). OUTNUMBERED BONUS: your gauge fills 25%
faster for each player targeting you (up to double).
On screen: thin threads in each sender's colour from the box's edge to their target (brighter as their gauge fills); the
"!" over your SOUL (with its sound) when a new player locks onto you; ON YOU: n; your shards and their POWER; in the player
list each player's number, shards, a yellow frame on your targets, a red ! on players targeting you, TIRED, and the name of
the player with the least HP pulsing. A battle-text line ("* Susie's gaze sharpens on you.") when a player targeting you
has 80+ charge.
The host's TARGETING page: TARGETING (off = every attack skill at every rival, as before build 48 - also the UNTARGETED
preset), each mode on or off, SHARDS, OUTNUMBERED BONUS. Bots pick their own mode for each send (build 49).

CHAT
----
T or \ opens it anywhere in a room, ENTER too during a match; ENTER or \ sends (the CHAT key can be changed in
KEYBINDS), ESC closes, TAB makes it bigger, the mouse wheel
scrolls. While you type, your keys are the chat's (your SOUL stays where it is and can still be hit; the match never
pauses). 80 characters a message, 5 messages in 5 seconds, the same text not twice in 4 seconds. Joins, leaves, the match's
start and end and the outs are announced. /mute NAME hides a player's messages for you, /unmute NAME shows them again.
The host keeps the last 30 messages for anyone who joins. QUICK SETTINGS > CHAT (or MATCH VIEW > CHAT) hides it on your
screen. A controller cannot type yet (use a keyboard for chat).

Watching (OUT, or SPECTATE MATCH from the room)
-----------------------------------------------
Watchers see the very battle the players see, from the same timeline; nothing a watcher does reaches the match.
  < > / LB RB    follow the next / previous living player       TAB (held)  every player
  Z              camera: FOLLOW (one player), FULL ARENA (every SOUL alike), AUTO (the final two, a skill, the lowest
                 HP, the leader), ZOOM (a lens: arrows move it, X 2x / 3x)
  C              watching card FULL / SMALL / OFF    V  only the followed SOUL for now    N  names for now
  I              MATCH STATS (time, damage taken, healed, grazes, skills, place)    H  the keys box
  F              QUICK SETTINGS            BACKSPACE (GIVE UP key)  back to the room
When the player you follow goes OUT the camera moves to the next living player by itself. When the match ends watchers get
the results; READY in the room is the rematch vote.

Controls in the match (the keys are yours: MORE OPTIONS > KEYBINDS, or KEYBINDS in the room)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  arrows        move your SOUL (MIRROR CONTROLS turns them around)
  X (held)      the game's own slow walk
  S             HOLD BREATH (build 50: a toggle - press to hold your breath, press again to let go): your SOUL moves
                faster while it is on, as in the fights (one speed faster; two during the Roaring Knight's attacks)
  C (held)      charge: the charge climbs up to your gauge (the TP bar's red part shows it); let go past the draw cost
                (60) for a skill, at 100 for two
  Z             tap: pick the next skill; hold (12 frames): use the picked one
  D             the picked skill into the HOLD slot, and back
  TAB (held)    every player in full
  F             QUICK SETTINGS: other SOULs on / off, SOUL OPACITY (slider), names, highlight, (watching: camera, HUD),
                CHAT, SFX VOLUME, MUSIC VOLUME. Your SOUL stays still while it is open.
  Q / E         TARGETING mode left / right        A  CHOOSE the next player       1-8  CHOOSE that player
  T or \        chat (ENTER or \ sends; ENTER also opens it in a match)
  V             OTHER SOULS: show / hide the others' SOULs at once (kept for your next match)
  BACKSPACE     give up (OUT, back to the room - it asks first) / stop watching
A controller: confirm = Z, menu button = C, LT = Hold Breath, RT = HOLD slot, LB / RB = targeting mode, right stick =
the next / previous target, BACK = player list, START = QUICK SETTINGS.
KEYBINDS: Z on an action, then the key you want (ESC cancels); a key another action had: the two swap. The arrows, X /
SHIFT, ENTER, ESC, F4 and 1-8 stay as they are. RESET KEYS puts S, Z, C, D, TAB, BACKSPACE, F, V, Q, E and A back.
A build 47 settings file that had HOLD SLOT on A and QUICK SETTINGS on Q is moved to D and F once.
The battle's own command menu and the party's panel never come up during an online match.

Skills, Settings, Steam, the relay
----------------------------------
As in build 46 (see the build 46 notes below), except that with TARGETING on a RED skill goes to your targets; GREEN
skills help you; the host's settings (PRESETS, GENERAL, KNIGHTS, KNIGHT RUSH, ATTACKS, SKILLS, SUDDEN DEATH, MUTATORS;
GENERAL > SPECTATORS lets room members watch, WATCH PLACES 0-4 extra places for them). Steam rooms run as Steam's test app
480 ("Spacewar"): kf_steam.dll, steam_api64.dll and steam_appid.txt stay in chapter3_windows; Steam must be running and
signed in. The relay: knightfights_server.zip (Node.js 18+, TCP 7777); everyone types its address in MORE OPTIONS > SERVER.

Known issues and limits (build 51 update 9 - DRBR)
--------------------------------------------------
- THE FIVE BOSSES (Jevil, Gerson, Flowery, Spamton NEO, Pink), their SOUL modes, their sends, the BOSSES and SKINS pages and
  the new skins were tested in the emulator that runs the mod's real code (TESTING_REPORT.txt, BUILD 51 UPDATE 9), NOT in
  the real game yet. Things only the real game can show: how each boss looks next to Chapter 3's HUD, Flowery's tower
  shaders (taken from Chapter 5), the bosses' music switching, the yellow / orange SOUL feel at the real frame rate. Please
  try each boss in PRACTICE first (ONLINE > BOSSES > Z) and send the whole error text of anything that stops.
- The bosses' music is read from DELTARUNE's shared mus folder (joker.ogg, ch4_extra_boss.ogg, Flowerman_Arrangement.ogg,
  spamton_neo_mix_ex_wip.ogg, pink.ogg): an install without Chapters 1-5 has no music for that boss.
- Bots cannot shoot, dash or change lanes like a player: Spamton NEO's mail walls, Flowery's board and Pink's tunnel and
  maze are played by the odds for them (a roll per hazard, by their skill).
- Everyone in a room needs this update (another build is told so and cannot join).

Known issues and limits (build 50)
----------------------------------
- Build 50's real attacks, TP bar, shield and Twin Knight were tested in the emulator that runs the mod's real code
  (TESTING_REPORT.txt, BUILD 50), not yet in the real game. Collision there is a stand-in, so the exact hitboxes of the
  sent attacks (the Knights' own masks) are the game's own and only checked in play.
- A send that lands while the box is gone between two attacks waits for the box; what reads the box (diagonal diamonds,
  weird circles, the slash circle) ends with it.
- The Twin Knight's real attacks, the Roaring HUD and the bots' Roaring rules were tested in the emulator (TESTING_REPORT.txt,
  BUILD 50 UPDATE), not in the real game.
- During the Roaring a sent star's hit is an ordinary hit (the Roaring routes the Knight's own star hits through the
  Knight).
- The slash triangles and the lightning are drawn in their own colours with the sender's colour mixed in only where the
  game's code takes a colour (sprites, sword colour, Violet's colour).
- Build 49 was tested in the emulator that runs the mod's real code, now with each game's camera where the real arena's
  is (TESTING_REPORT.txt, BUILD 49), and the real relay server; it has not been played in the real game yet, and Steam
  rooms are still not confirmed on real Steam. If the other SOULs still do not show, press F3 in the match and send a
  screenshot plus knightfights_debug.log (%LOCALAPPDATA%\DELTARUNE). Please try
  PRACTICE first, then a room with a friend, and send the whole error text of anything that stops.
- Everyone in a room needs build 50 (another build is told so and cannot join).
- The SOULs, the chat panel, the targeting row, the threads and the GAME OVER were checked in the emulator's own pictures,
  not on a real screen.
- Chat needs a keyboard (no on-screen keyboard for a controller yet).
- In YOUR game a send at ANOTHER player starts where the host last saw their SOUL (up to about a tenth of a second old);
  in the target's own game it is exactly their SOUL.
- Steam avatars are not shown (each player's skin and SOUL colour are).
- Accepting a Steam invite while the game is closed starts Spacewar (app 480): start DELTARUNE first, or use the code.
- Each game decides its own hits, so a game someone changed could cheat on its own HP (the host catches only impossible
  numbers).
- A player whose game dropped out of a running match cannot play on in it (with REJOIN or SPECTATORS on they can watch it).
- In the cold place other players are drawn above the scenery (no depth sorting with trees); bots do not walk around it.
- The bots only exist in the host's game: the host's PC does their thinking (at most 6 ms of a frame for all of them;
  a bot that runs out of time keeps its move for that frame). Their dodging was measured in the emulator with a stand-in
  for GameMaker's collision masks (the real sprites' boxes); on the real screen they may do a little better (the real
  masks are smaller than those boxes). Some attacks cannot be fully dodged by anyone (a slash that appears on the SOUL):
  even PERFECT takes those hits.

Build 46 notes kept for reference: Hold Breath used to halve damage and double grazes (build 46) - it is now the fights'
faster SOUL. The rest of build 46's guide (skills list, settings list, Steam troubleshooting) still applies.
