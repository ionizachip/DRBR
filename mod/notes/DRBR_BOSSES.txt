DRBR - DELTARUNE BATTLE ROYALE: THE BOSSES AND HOW CLOSE THEY ARE TO THE ORIGINAL GAME
Knight Hub v2, build 51 (update 9). Online only: offline DELTARUNE and the offline Knight Hub are not changed by any of this.

WHERE THE BOSSES COME FROM
Every boss below is built from the game's own code and art, taken from the player's own DELTARUNE files when the patch is
built (Chapter 1, 2, 4 and 5 data.win, decompiled with UndertaleModTool). Nothing is redrawn or rewritten from memory: the
attack pieces are the original objects renamed with a prefix (obj_jv_* Jevil, obj_hj_* Gerson, obj_fl_* Flowery, obj_sn_*
Spamton NEO, obj_pk_* Pink) so they cannot clash with Chapter 3's own objects, and their code is changed only where the
shared online match needs it (listed per boss). The surveys that mapped each fight before it was ported are in
online/notes/src_gerson.md, src_flowery.md, src_spamton.md and src_pink.md (Jevil: JEVIL_SPEC.txt).

WHAT IS THE SAME FOR EVERY BOSS (the online rules, and why)
- ONE SHARED FIGHT. In DRBR every player in the room fights the same boss at the same time, each in their own game. So each
  boss's turn order is kept (the original fixed script where the game has one), but every attack starts and ends on the same
  tick in every game: the attack's length is the original turn timer, measured in the emulator with nobody touching a key.
- NO MENU TURNS. There is no FIGHT / ACT / SPARE / ITEM between attacks online, so the fights' talk-and-mercy parts (Jevil's
  "chaos" ACTs, Gerson's progress meter, Flowery's ACTs, Pink's DOKI dates, Spamton's deals) are replaced by the room's
  stages: a boss moves to its next phase as the match goes on (Flowery: through the GROUP MERCY, below). The bosses' own
  endings (Gerson's haircut, Flowery's climb, Pink's confession, Spamton's [BIG SHOT] ending) are not part of a match.
- DAMAGE goes through the room's settings (DAMAGE %, the knights' table) instead of each chapter's party stats; an attack
  that took a share of everyone's HP in its chapter (Pink's bombs, Jevil's laser scythe) is one normal hit.
- RANDOMNESS. What decides an attack's shape (which pattern, where pieces appear) comes from the match's shared random
  stream, so every game sees the same attack. What a player DOES (a parry, a shot, a block, a kicked bomb) uses the piece's
  own stream, so one player's play never changes another player's bullets.
- THE SOUL MODES are per player: your SOUL turns green / yellow / orange / purple only in your own game, for that boss's
  attacks, and turns red again after. A cosmetic SOUL COLOR never replaces a mode's colour.
- SENDS: each boss has a family of attacks a player can send at another (SKILLS, the red ones). A send is built around the
  TARGET's SOUL in the target's game only, from the same original objects, scaled so it stays dodgeable on top of the boss's
  own attack (smaller, fewer, slower where needed); nobody else is hurt by it.
- The boss music is the game's own file from the shared DELTARUNE "mus" folder.
- A boss whose art or code is missing from a copy of the game is never offered (it is greyed out and named "missing" in
  the BOSSES page; presets that need it are hidden).

JEVIL - Chapter 1's secret boss (obj_joker, encounter 25)  [SECRET BOSS, RED SOUL]
Taken as it is: his 16 attacks in his own order (obj_dbulletcontroller types 70 65 49 75 62 50 73 68 61 48 72 76 71 46 74
77), their 15 piece objects, 29 sprites, 10 sounds, his body (idle, dance, shadow dance, sinking into the box, becoming the
Devilsknife), his lines, the carousel background and THE WORLD REVOLVING (joker.ogg). Phases: his turns 0-3, then 0-7, then
all; BYE-BYE is his finale in the Roaring's place.
Changed: the SOUL's middle (+10, Chapter 3's SOUL), Chapter 1's view enum replaced by Chapter 3's camera (63 reads, update

Changed: Chapter 5's ACT / MERCY (POSEY, BLOWAWAY, SPIN, PRAISE, JUSTICE, SUSIE'S IDEA) became the GROUP MERCY - each game

Sends (7, only in a match Flowery is in - they are pieces of his board): a Jarona copy, bamboo, Aqua's knives, Seth's books,

HOW THIS WAS CHECKED (all in the emulator - the real merged code running in a GameMaker stand-in, NOT the real game)
Per boss: every attack in its own practice match (it starts on the shared tick, makes its pieces, ends by its own turn timer,
leaves nothing behind, the SOUL mode turns on and off, no error); a player who plays (dashes, shoots, blocks, changes lanes)
and one who does not; three games over the simulated network (the same attacks, the same bullets where nobody plays, no
desync); every send built only in the target's game and hurting only the target; six bots per boss (from DUMMY to PERFECT:
more skill, fewer hits); all bosses in one rush (Knight Rush between every boss, nothing left from the last one). The
numbers are in TESTING_REPORT.txt (BUILD 51 UPDATE 9). Not checked: how each one looks and feels in the real game - the
emulator does not draw shaders (Flowery's tower) or surfaces the way the GPU does, and its timing is frame-exact rather than
real time.
