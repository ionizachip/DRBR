DRBR (DELTARUNE BATTLE ROYALE) - THE ONLINE SKINS, AND ADDING ONE
==================================================================

Every skin is cosmetic online: the same HP, stats, speed, SOUL and hitbox for everyone (the online kit), whatever you wear.
Skins 1-5 (Kris, Susie, Ralsei, Noelle, Flowery) are the hub's own heroes - their own battle art. Skins 6 and up are LOOKS
drawn over Kris. Every look exists only in online / practice rooms: the hub's own game, its party, its story and offline
DELTARUNE never see them (offline you are Kris; choosing a skin never makes you a boss or changes a fight).

THE SKINS (14)
  PARTY    1 Kris, 2 Susie, 3 Ralsei, 4 Noelle (the game's own)
  FLOWERS  5 Flowery (the hub's own hero), 7 Aqua, 8 Seth, 9 Orange, 10 Green, 11 Yellow, 12 Blue
           (Chapter 5's six colored flowers, the ones its own scr_miniface_init_flowers names; walking in all four facings
           from Chapter 5's own walk sprites)
  BOSSES   13 Pink (Chapter 5: spr_pink_walk_* in four facings, battle spr_pk_pink_idle), 14 Spamton (Chapter 2's normal,
           small Spamton - NOT Spamton NEO: spr_spamton_jump hopping as his walk, spr_spamton_idle standing still,
           spr_spamtonhead as his face)
  OTHER    6 Berdly (Chapter 2)

THE SKINS PAGE (ONLINE > SKIN, or the room's SKIN row: Z)
  < > the group (PARTY / FLOWERS / BOSSES / OTHER), up / down (or the mouse wheel) a skin, Z wears it and goes back, X goes
  back as you were, C puts Kris back. The preview shows how it walks in the cold place (round its four facings), its
  battle pose, its face when it has one, and whether it is a hero of the game or a look. On the ONLINE page and in the
  room, < > on the SKIN row still changes it right there. In a running match you change it between matches.

ADDING A SKIN
Everything about a skin is ONE entry in kfo_skin_defs() (scripts/gml_templates_v6/online_skin52.gml):

  { num: 15, key: "name", name: "Name", own: false, bchar: 1, cat: "other",
    ow: [down, right, up, left],        overworld walking sprites (4 frames each, like Kris's)
    ow_flip: [f, f, f, f],              optional: mirror a facing whose art only faces the other way (Spamton's right)
    ow_idle: [d, r, u, l],              optional: a standing sprite per facing (a look whose walk is not a step cycle)
    bt: "spr_...", bt_flip: false,      battle idle - must FACE RIGHT (the party side); bt_flip: true only for art that
                                        faces left and has no right-facing version. Check the sprite, never assume.
    bt_spd: 0.2,                        battle frames per game step
    portrait: "spr_...", port_img: 0,   dialogue face for the menus (OPTIONAL: leave it out and none is drawn - never
    port_sc: 1, port_dark: false,       use another character's). port_sc: whole-number scale.
    head: [x, y, w, h], head_spr: "",   crop used as the small head (HP panel, lists); head_spr: the sprite to crop it from
                                        when it is not the first down-walking frame
    col: make_color_rgb(...),           UI colour (HP bar, name, selection, portrait frame) - take it from the art
    col2: make_color_rgb(...) }         second accent
  cat: the SKINS page's group - "main", "flowers", "bosses" or "other".

Then:
  1. Put the sprites in the build: scripts/add_skin_assets.py (add(src, 'sprites', name)). Donors: c2 = Chapter 2,
     c5 = Chapter 5, base = Chapter 3 (already in the game, nothing to copy).
  2. kfo_skin_count() + 1.
  3. Optional: kbr_tg_callout (online_target.gml) - two lines for when they target you.
  4. Build. A missing sprite shows MISSING ART on the SKINS page and the look falls back to Kris (logged once in
     knightfights_debug.log) - it never crashes.

Network: a skin is a number (the "skin" message, the lobby's "s"). A game that does not know a number shows Kris.

Test: t_online_ui47.py (the SKINS page with every skin, online and in a room), the cold-place renders (every look, every
facing, walking and standing - yours and the other players'), then a real room with two games.
