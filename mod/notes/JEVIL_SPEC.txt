KNIGHT FIGHTS ONLINE - JEVIL AND THE SECRET BOSS SLOTS
(the "Full Jevil Implementation" prompt, rewritten to fit how Knight Fights works today - build 51)
=========================================================================================================

WHAT KNIGHT FIGHTS ALREADY IS (so the work builds on it, not beside it)
 - Every player's game runs its own copy of the same battle. Nothing is streamed per bullet. A match is a TIMELINE of
   attack CYCLES that every game works out for itself from the host's seed and the room's settings (kbr_tl_*): cycle k =
   which encounter, which attack, its seed, the tick it starts, how long it runs, the stage, Twin/Hyper, final two, sudden
   death. The host only checks that games stay in step (cycle signatures, the shared clock) and passes players' states.
 - Attack randomness comes from a stream seeded per attack (kbr_random / kbr_choose / kbr_irandom - the attack code calls
   these instead of random()), so unaimed parts land in the same place in every game; aimed parts aim at your own SOUL.
 - The "encounter director" is the timeline plus the LINEUP: the encounters of a match are made once and switched in and
   out between attacks (knight_lu_*, kbr_step/kbr_arm/kbr_cut). An attack is armed (box up, SOUL centred), held at the
   shared tick (kbr_rgate), runs, and is cut on the same frame in every game.
 - The ROSTER (kbr_roster) is one table of online encounters: an online id is its row; rows are only ever added at the
   end; MATCH SETTINGS (which encounters, weights, the rushes) and the HUD names/colours follow from the table. It was made
   for "the bosses of the other chapters".
 - Damage never comes from the bullet: every hit goes through scr_damage -> the room's damage table by kind of bullet
   (kbr_hit_kind / kbr_hit_dmg) and the room's DAMAGE setting. One HP total per player; going to 0 = OUT, watching.
 - Player attacks ("skills", red) are the bosses' REAL attack objects wrapped (kbr_sa / online_sent50): built around the
   target's SOUL in the target's game, invisible copies in the others' games for the bots and the watchers, only the
   recipient hurt. Targeting is the Tetris 99-style row (CHAOS, PROCEED, BIG SHOT, DEFEND, CHECK, PACIFY, ROARING).
 - Bots dodge whatever is in the box (generic bullet tracking) - a new boss needs no bot code.
 - Room settings are pages (online_set.gml), presets are named setting sets (kfo_set presets).

THE REQUEST, IN THOSE TERMS
1. ENCOUNTERS. The roster gets a kind: KNIGHT (the existing rows) or SECRET BOSS. Jevil is the first secret boss. A small
   encounter table (kbr_enc_*) holds what a non-knight needs: key, name, short name, colour, category, music file, its
   attack list in the knights' format [phase, id, name, flags], each attack's length, its finale (its "Roaring"), the
   lineup id (1000 + n: never a hub registry index, so the offline hub is untouched), and the assets it needs (a boss whose
   assets are missing is never valid - never shown, never picked). Adding the next boss = a table entry, its objects, a
   roster row. Unfinished bosses are not listed.
2. JEVIL'S OWN FIGHT, FROM CHAPTER 1. The player's Chapter 1 data.win is the source: obj_joker's attacks are 16
   obj_dbulletcontroller types (70, 65, 49, 75, 62, 50, 73, 68, 61, 48, 72, 76, 71, 46, 74, 77) that make obj_spadering,
   obj_suitbomb (spade/diamond/heart/club bombs), obj_heartbomb_blast, obj_carouselbullet, obj_centerscythe,
   obj_laserscythe, obj_bigscythe, obj_joker_teleport, obj_clubsbullet_dark, obj_dbullet_vert. They are imported as their
   own objects (obj_jv_*: Chapter 3 has some of these names for other things), with their sprites and Jevil's voice
   sounds, and their code changed only where Chapter 3 differs: the SOUL's middle is +10 (Chapter 1: +8), randomness
   through the attack stream, obj_joker -> Jevil's battle object, damage through the room's table (the laser scythe's
   "take 30% of everyone's HP" becomes a normal hit).
   Jevil's look: obj_joker_body's drawing (idle, dance, tired, the shadow-dance, sinking into the box for the attacks that
   need it, coming back, turning into the devilsknife), its sounds (CHAOS, CHAOS!, I CAN DO ANYTHING!, BYE-BYE!, the oh's).
   The background: obj_jokerbg_triangle_real as Chapter 1 draws it - the carousel picture in 32 strips (a turning drum),
   scrolling, the canopy's navy triangles turning, fading in when Jevil steps in and out when he leaves, at depth 600000
   (behind the battle). No shader is involved. The Knight's own background is hidden while Jevil is out.
   Music: joker.ogg (THE WORLD REVOLVING) from DELTARUNE's own mus folder, while Jevil is out; the match's song after.
3. JEVIL IN THE TIMELINE. Phases keep Chapter 1's order: phase 1 = attacks 0-3, phase 2 = 4-7, phase 3 = 8-11 and
   12-14; stage 0 draws from phase 1, stage 1 from phases 1-2, stage 2+ from all (12-14 with HARD ATTACKS on), as for a
   knight. His finale (attack 15, BYE-BYE: the box goes, devilsknives rain, the giant scythe and the white-out) takes the
   Roaring's place (3 minutes in, the final two). His dance level follows the stage. Each attack's length comes from its
   own turn timer (240, 270, 300, 330 frames; the finale until it ends). Everyone fights the same Jevil, the same attack,
   at the same tick; a swap between a knight and Jevil cleans the other one's bullets, background and music.
4. JEVIL SKILLS (what players send). New red skills built from Jevil's own pieces, through kbr_sa like the knights'
   skills (around the target's SOUL, only the target hurt, the others see nothing that hurts them):
     SPADE RING (spade ring, low cost), DIAMOND RAIN (falling diamonds, mid), CLUB SPIRAL (the dark clubs closing in,
     mid), HEART CAROUSEL (the carousel horses, high), DEVILSKNIFE (the spinning scythe, high), CHAOS BOMBS (a few suit
     bombs, high). Their cost, damage and pool follow the existing skill table (kbr_skill_*): low / mid / full pools.
   They go into the draw pool only when the room's SKILL FAMILIES allows them.
5. PRESETS. One new row in MATCH SETTINGS: ENCOUNTERS = KNIGHTS / JEVIL / SECRET BOSSES / ALL BOSSES / CUSTOM (custom =
   the existing per-encounter rows). One row: SKILL FAMILIES = KNIGHT / JEVIL / ALL. Secret Bosses = every completed
   secret boss (Jevil now). Settings go to everyone with the room as they do today.
6. FAIRNESS. Room difficulty (DAMAGE, HARD ATTACKS, the stage ramp, Hyper) applies to Jevil as to the knights. Sends
   already have their own pressure limits (cooldown, slots, outnumbered bonus).
7. EVERYTHING ELSE STAYS: watching (followed player's view), other SOULs, chat, the HUD, bots, OUT and shatter, results.
   Offline DELTARUNE and the offline hub do not change (Jevil is online only; the Cyan Knight stays out of online play).
8. TESTS (emulated: the real code, a mocked engine; nothing here is the real game): every Jevil attack runs to its end
   and leaves nothing behind; the timeline picks Jevil only when the room allows it and in the right phase order; three
   games over the simulated network see the same Jevil cycles; a swap knight -> Jevil -> knight leaves no stale objects,
   background or music; each Jevil skill reaches only its target; the presets; the existing tests still pass.
