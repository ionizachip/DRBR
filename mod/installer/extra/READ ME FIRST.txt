KNIGHT HUB - build 51 - TESTER PACK
===================================
NEW HERE? Open EASY INSTALL GUIDE.txt first: four steps, including the save in "Save file" (Chapter 3 slot 3, at the
Knight Hub) so you can start right away.

A mod for DELTARUNE Chapter 3 (Steam, Windows). It puts all the Roaring Knight fights in one menu (a sign in the
Roaring Knight area), plus DRBR - DELTARUNE BATTLE ROYALE: a battle royale for 2-8 players where everyone dodges the SAME
boss, the SAME attacks, at the SAME moment, and the last SOUL standing wins - the Knights, and (online) Jevil, Gerson,
Flowery, Spamton NEO and Pink with their own SOULs. This build is for testing: things may break, and that is what to
report.


WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
 - DELTARUNE from Steam on Windows, Chapter 3. The installer only accepts the exact Chapter 3 data.win this build was
   made for. If yours is a different version or already modded, it stops without changing anything ("exit code 6",
   or PLAY_KNIGHT_HUB.bat saying it could not put the original data.win back). Then run Steam's "Verify integrity of
   game files" (below) and try again; if it still refuses, your DELTARUNE version is not the one this build was made
   for (README_INSTALL.txt, section 4).
 - A Chapter 3 save at the Roaring Knight area (the cold place). The hub is the sign there.
 - For Steam rooms: Steam running and signed in. (Nothing else to install: kf_steam.dll, steam_api64.dll and
   steam_appid.txt come with this pack and are copied next to the game by PLAY_KNIGHT_HUB.bat.)


INSTALL
-------
 1. Extract the whole zip into a folder. Do not run anything from inside the zip.
 2. Start Steam, then double-click PLAY_KNIGHT_HUB.bat.
    It finds DELTARUNE, copies these files into DELTARUNE\chapter3_windows, keeps a backup of your original data.win
    (data.win.vanilla), installs the mod (replacing an older Knight Hub build) and starts Chapter 3. If Windows asks
    for administrator rights, allow it (the game folder is usually under Program Files).
 3. Load your save at the Roaring Knight area and talk to the sign.

UNDO: double-click RESTORE_VANILLA.bat in DELTARUNE\chapter3_windows. It puts the original data.win back (the Knight Hub
files, the three Steam files too, stay in the folder: the normal game does not use them). Steam > DELTARUNE >
Properties > Installed Files > "Verify integrity of game files" also works.


DRBR - DELTARUNE BATTLE ROYALE (KNIGHT tab > ONLINE)
----------------------------------------------------
 PRACTICE       a whole match on your own PC against bots. Try this first.
 BOSSES         every boss with its SOUL and sends; Z starts a practice match against the one you pick. The room page's BOTS row adds / removes
                bots and sets their difficulty (DUMMY, EASY, NORMAL, HARD, EXPERT, PERFECT, or sliders per bot).
 STEAM HOST     make a Steam room (friends-only by default; MORE OPTIONS > STEAM ROOMS: private / friends / public).
                It shows a 7-letter code. Give it to the others, or INVITE FRIENDS from the room page.
 STEAM JOIN     type the 7-letter code.        STEAM BROWSE   the public Steam rooms.
 RELAY ...      the same rooms on a small server instead of Steam (see the server section below).
In the room: READY (the host: START), SPECTATE MATCH while a match runs, your SKIN and SOUL colour. Closing the menu keeps
you in the room: the cold place is the lobby - no party following you, everyone in their own skin, and the way to the
original Knight fight is closed while you are in a room. In a match (change the keys in MORE OPTIONS > KEYBINDS):
 arrows      move your SOUL              X (held)  the game's own slow walk
 S (held)    Hold Breath: your SOUL moves faster (as in the fights)
 C (held)    charge the gauge (the TP bar), let go to draw one skill (60) or two (100)
 Z (tap)     pick the next skill         Z (held)  use the picked skill
 D           the picked skill into the HOLD slot / the held one back
 F           quick settings                          V         the other SOULs off / on
 Q / E       TARGETING mode left / right             A / 1-8   pick a target (CHECK)
  T or \     chat (\ sends, ESC closes; not ENTER)   TAB       everyone's HP, shards and gauge
 Backspace   give up / stop watching           F3        DEBUG OVERLAY (anywhere; see below)
 Controller: LB / RB targeting mode, right stick a target, LT Hold Breath, RT the HOLD slot.
TARGETING (the row under the box): CHAOS, PROCEED, BIG SHOT, DEFEND, CHECK, PACIFY or ROARING decides who YOUR red skills
go to. They start around that player's SOUL, hurt anyone who touches them (not you), in your colour. A knockout gives you
Shadow Crystal shards (1 + half of theirs) that make your sends stronger. The host can switch targeting off (UNTARGETED).
The other players' SOULs are shown at 70% in their own colours: MORE OPTIONS > SOUL SETTINGS (five rows; SOUL COLOR opens
the colour page). When your SOUL breaks it cracks and shatters as in Chapter 3, the GAME OVER shows over the battle with
your place, then you watch (Left / Right: whose SOUL, Z: camera); the others' SOULs break where they were. The final two
and the first attack after 3 minutes are the Knight's Roaring. Everything is explained in DRBR_ONLINE_GUIDE.txt (the bosses: DRBR_BOSSES.txt).
Everyone in a room needs this same build (51).
Steam shows "Spacewar" while you are in Steam rooms - that is expected (Steam's test app 480 is what lets a mod make
rooms). If a friend accepts an invite while their game is closed, Steam starts Spacewar instead: close it, start
DELTARUNE and join with the code.


RUNNING THE RELAY SERVER (only if you use RELAY rooms instead of Steam; one person in the group)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 1. Install Node.js 18 or newer (https://nodejs.org, the LTS installer).
 2. Open the "Knight Fights Online server" folder and double-click START_RELAY.bat. Keep that window open.
 3. The others set MORE OPTIONS > SERVER to this PC's address:
      same PC                          127.0.0.1
      same network / Radmin / Hamachi  this PC's LAN or VPN address
      over the internet                forward TCP port 7777 to this PC, or use a tunnel such as playit.gg
                                       (details in README.md in the server folder)


WHAT TO SEND BACK
-----------------
 - A crash: the whole error text (a screenshot of the error window is fine).
 - Steam rooms do not work: what the ONLINE page says at the bottom right (READY / NOT INSTALLED / STEAM_API64.DLL
   MISSING / STEAM IS NOT RUNNING) and what happens when you press STEAM HOST.
 - The install fails: the text in the installer window, and knight_hub_install.log from DELTARUNE\chapter3_windows.
 - A match: did everyone see the same knight and attack at the same time? Anything out of step, too hard, too easy?
 - Which build is running: the bottom right of the sign's menu says "KNIGHT HUB V2 - BUILD 51 - <date>". If it says
   anything else (or nothing), the game did not load this build's data.win: run PLAY_KNIGHT_HUB.bat again.
 - The other SOULs or the bots are not where they should be: press F3 in the match (the DEBUG OVERLAY: every SOUL's
   position, drawn or not and why, a coloured square at each), take a screenshot, and send knightfights_debug.log from
   %LOCALAPPDATA%\DELTARUNE (written while the overlay is on).


HONEST STATUS
-------------
Build 51: a cleaner match screen. Your skills are diamonds at the top left with pictures (green plus = MAX, a full heal
everyone starts with once). The TARGETING modes have plain names (RANDOM, LOW HP, CRYSTALS, REVENGE, CHOOSE, TIRED,
EVERYONE) on one strip at the bottom. The other players are small cards on the right. NEW: HOW TO PLAY on the sign's
ONLINE page walks you through a practice match step by step. If you get knocked out without being hit, the results
screen says why - please send a screenshot of it and knightfights_debug.log (in %LOCALAPPDATA%\DELTARUNE).

Build 50: the attack skills players send each other are now the Roaring Knights' REAL attacks (the tracking sword, the
pointing stars, the rotating slash, Kaizo's diamonds, crescents and falling swords, Elecknight's lightning, Thorn's
fountain, the Violet Knight's eruption...), with five new items that only come when their knight is in the match. The
charge and item system is unchanged. The TP bar is the skill meter (a slanted 60 line, gold above it, MAX at 100, the red
part showing what a draw takes), your SHIELD shows in blue on your HP bar, Hold Breath is a toggle, shards use the
Shadow Crystal graphic, and the Twin Knight flies round the box doing its attacks - now with the Knights' real attacks
(stars that burst into starchildren, tracking swords, falling swords), the Knight's warp, poses, afterimages and sounds,
and see-through. The HUD steps aside during a knight's Roaring, and the bots no longer always die in it. Flowery (Chapter 5)
is a party member of his own (his own hero, stats, gear and golden HP bar - he no longer takes Noelle's place - with moves
from his own Chapter 5 fight, F-Action and voice clips, with every knight), Flower Man / Raise Up Your Bat show their
lyrics during the fight, your SKIN stays after you close the menu, and practice starts with no bots. All of it was
tested in the emulator that runs the mod's real code - NOT in the real game yet.

Build 49 fixes the reason nobody saw the other SOULs or the bots in the real game: the Knight fight takes place far into
the cold place (the camera is at x 2063 and more), and build 48 cut every SOUL position it passed on at x 1000 (drawn off
screen) and took the real battle box for "no box" (the bots never moved and were never drawn). Every position now travels
relative to the battle's camera. Our earlier tests ran the battle with the camera at 0, where this never showed; the new
test puts each game's camera where the real arena's is, and the same test run on build 48's code shows the old failure.
Build 49 also brings smart bots with six difficulty presets and sliders, the BOTS page, the build stamp, the F3 DEBUG
OVERLAY with its log, an opacity floor of 15% and CONFIG > RESET MOD SETTINGS.
Build 49 has NOT been played in the real game, and Steam rooms have NOT been confirmed on real Steam. Expect rough edges
and please report them (TESTING_REPORT.txt lists exactly what was tested and what was not).
